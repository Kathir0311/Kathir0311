Split a two-page spread

The left half stays this page and the right half becomes the next page, so a screen reader reads the left page and then the right page. Nothing printed changes: each new page simply shows its own half. Every tag goes with its half and every later page moves up by one; contents links are searched again so they land on the right sheet.

Colours are measured again afterwards, and Undo starts afresh — use “Put the last split back” to return to the sheet as it was.
