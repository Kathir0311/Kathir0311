    Our own AI

    Meet DezAI — the AI that lives on your server.

    Dez AI is the studio’s own AI: a local language and vision model together with a self-learning brain that is trained on your approved files. It proposes every tag, drafts every alt text, explains its choice and learns from every correction — and nothing you give it ever leaves your network.

    • Tags a whole file in one pass

      Headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, figures, captions, links, footnotes, contents and reading order — proposed together.

    • Writes the alt text

      A draft description for every picture from the local vision model; decorative ones become artifacts; a picture described once is remembered.

    • Learns from your corrections — live

      Change a tag or cut a zone once. A few seconds later Dez AI offers the same fix everywhere in the file and uses it on the next files of that client.

    • Knows each client’s style

      Learns the house rules from approved files — what is a heading, where a paragraph ends — and keeps one convention inside a file.

    • Shows its reasons

      Every proposal says how sure it is and why; the doubtful tags are listed first, so your time goes where it matters.

    • Private by design

      Dez AI refuses every connection outside your network. No cloud service, no tracker, no fonts from the internet.

    Why teams choose it

    Everything an accessibility team needs, in one studio.

    • PDF/UA and WCAG, checked

      ISO 14289-1 machine checks on every build, colour-contrast checks with one-click repair, optional veraPDF, and an Excel report for every file.

    • An editor that feels like Acrobat — and is faster

      Draw a zone and press a key, drag the reading order, merge and split tags, edit tables and lists, undo and redo — with hotkeys for everything.

    • Links, in one stage

      Cross-references, footnotes, contents, figures, tables, references, web and e-mail links found for you — one-way or two-way — checked and tagged.

    • Hear it the way NVDA reads it

      Say-all with the words lit as they are spoken, quick keys, and every problem a listener would trip over — before your client does.

    • Batch, fast, multi-core

      Upload files or whole folders and let the queue run: several files at once, every page analysed on all your cores, and everybody served in turn.

    • House styles for every client

      Profiles per client, learned from their approved files, so a publisher’s books come out the way that publisher wants them — every time.

    • Scanned pages and many languages

      Optional OCR gives scanned pages a text layer; Unicode text — Tamil, Greek and more — is read, tagged and spoken correctly.

    • Made for teams

      Roles, assignments, due dates, PAC reports, review comments and a history of every step — and one person at a time in a file, so nobody’s work is overwritten.

    How it works

    From upload to delivery in four steps.

    1. 1Upload

      One file, many files or a whole folder. Pick the client’s profile — or let DezAI choose by the look of the file.

    2. 2Dez AI tags it

      The structure, the reading order and the alt texts are proposed in one pass, with a confidence for every tag.

    3. 3You review and fix

      Look at the doubtful tags first. Every correction teaches Dez AI — live, not at the end of the day.

    4. 4Build, check, deliver

      The accessible PDF is built, checked against PDF/UA and WCAG, and delivered with its report.
